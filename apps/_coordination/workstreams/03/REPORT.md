### Capture rate versus Meteor LRPT decode — REG-06 OPEN

- [Native scheduling](https://github.com/Tihulu/TIHULU-TEXT-CENTER/blob/670e0ddcf029c8fcc9b0cef403bf76cf37613fcb/satellite_center/__main__.py#L881) and [raw capture](https://github.com/Tihulu/TIHULU-TEXT-CENTER/blob/670e0ddcf029c8fcc9b0cef403bf76cf37613fcb/satellite_center/__main__.py#L1556) support **1,024,000 or 2,048,000 samples/s** and use 2×rate×seconds CU8 bytes with a **2 GB** cap. IQ [capture filenames](https://github.com/Tihulu/TIHULU-TEXT-CENTER/blob/670e0ddcf029c8fcc9b0cef403bf76cf37613fcb/satellite_center/__main__.py#L1600) include the rate in `_{sample_rate}SPS`, and [capture results](https://github.com/Tihulu/TIHULU-TEXT-CENTER/blob/670e0ddcf029c8fcc9b0cef403bf76cf37613fcb/satellite_center/__main__.py#L1645) return `sample_rate`. However [`decode_meteor()`](https://github.com/Tihulu/TIHULU-TEXT-CENTER/blob/670e0ddcf029c8fcc9b0cef403bf76cf37613fcb/satellite_center/__main__.py#L1773) **unconditionally invokes SatDump with `--samplerate 1024000`**, without checking this saved rate. 2.048-MS/s material cannot be claimed decoded correctly. Legacy recordings without trustworthy metadata must remain **unknown-rate**, not silently certified 1.024. A synthetic captured **300 s CU8** must contain 614,400,000 bytes at 1.024 or 1,228,800,000 bytes at 2.048; a **900 s** capture at 2.048 is 3,686,400,000 bytes and exceeds the allowed 2 GB (must reject).
- **Failure honesty:** [`decode_meteor()`](https://github.com/Tihulu/TIHULU-TEXT-CENTER/blob/670e0ddcf029c8fcc9b0cef403bf76cf37613fcb/satellite_center/__main__.py#L1786) uses `dest.mkdir(exist_ok=True)` and recursively selects PNG/JPG entries without gating on `result.returncode`. A *previous run's* unprefixed SatDump PNG could be mistaken for output of a **new failed decode**: this is a **source-inferred potential stale-artifact issue, not a reproduced incident**. The method emits `no_images_decoded` when no image exists and only a 500-byte tail of stderr.

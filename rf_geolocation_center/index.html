Radio positioning · antenna only

Use RTL-SDR RF observations without using the device's GPS, Wi-Fi, IP location or browser Geolocation API. Fixed transmitter coordinates come from an explicitly entered external source. I/Q level is measured; distance is only an experimental propagation-model estimate after site-specific calibration.

Automatic RF source discovery

Look up real published radio transmitter sites automatically (OpenStreetMap/Overpass). Their reported positions and frequencies are external catalog candidates, not proof your antenna received those stations. Select an approximate search region; it is not used as your measured position. Manual inputs remain available below.

Calibration at a known transmitter distance

With the antenna at a known distance from the selected fixed transmitter, capture its RF level first, enter the independently known distance, then save calibration. At an unknown site capture the *same source* again using identical fixed gain, bandwidth and antenna. This estimates range only under a simplified power-loss assumption. It does not identify the transmitter signal or provide reliable distance without validation.

No RTL-SDR measurement yet. Connect the USB SDR in the Receiver panel, then scan a chosen source.

Scientific limit: RF intensity does not uniquely determine distance. Terrain, multipath, antenna patterns, gain, co-channel interference and source power affect this test. Aircraft and satellites appear on independent RF layers, but are not treated as fixed ground transmitters in this 2D RSSI solver.

HARDWARE & PRIVACY

Permission-controlled local operation

Capabilities

Native SDR GNSS decoding, raw IQ capture, GNSS-SDR/RTK backend, autonomous background sessions and multi-client network receiver control are not provided by the browser-only version.

Stop and storage

Hard Stop cancels this app's own Web Serial reader, releases any locally claimed interfaces, removes live device handles and stops active operations. It does not kill readsb, Gqrx, other browser tabs or unrelated radio software.

No telemetry, cloud analytics or automatic background RF or orbital downloads. Service worker caches only same-origin application shell assets, never OSM tiles or private imported observation files.

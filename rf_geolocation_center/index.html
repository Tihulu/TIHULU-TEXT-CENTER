What actually determines my location?

Browser/device GPS: real positioning after permission. Dedicated GNSS receiver: valid live NMEA fixes. Predicted satellites: orbital geometry only, not reception. Aircraft and radio stations: positions alone cannot locate a receiver without independent range, timing or directional measurements. WebUSB RTL-SDR: direct hardware connection and raw RF energy samples where supported, but no GNSS satellite signal decoder yet.

EXPERIMENTAL RF GEOMETRY

Radio positioning

Only ranges from genuine calibrated measurements qualify. RSSI alone cannot produce a defensible distance. No unverified results are labeled GNSS FIX.

HARDWARE & PRIVACY

Permission-controlled local operation

Capabilities

Native SDR GNSS decoding, raw IQ capture, GNSS-SDR/RTK backend, autonomous background sessions and multi-client network receiver control are not provided by the browser-only version.

Stop and storage

Hard Stop cancels this app's own Web Serial reader, releases any locally claimed interfaces, removes live device handles and stops active operations. It does not kill readsb, Gqrx, other browser tabs or unrelated radio software.

No telemetry, cloud analytics or automatic background RF or orbital downloads. Service worker caches only same-origin application shell assets, never OSM tiles or private imported observation files.

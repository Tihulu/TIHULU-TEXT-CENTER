A permission grant and a working USB interface are different. 4 · Scheduled reception & RF sample analysis

To schedule: 1. Calculate passes → 2. select Schedule / configure on a pass → 3. choose the exact local date/minute, seconds, frequency and recording → 4. confirm → 5. grant USB access once and keep this browser tab open, visible and awake until the scheduled time.

At the selected time the browser will attempt to reopen a previously permitted RTL-SDR, sample raw I/Q and calculate genuine relative power statistics. An optional IQ file download is attempted. This is not Meteor image decoding, APRS text decoding or identification of an individual satellite. An antenna and an active transmission are needed for real reception.

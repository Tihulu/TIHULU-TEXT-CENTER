1 · Capture a real downlink

Use the Nooelec NESDR SMArt v5 RTL2832U for compatible VHF/UHF receive-only downlinks. A suitable external antenna and a visible, transmitting satellite are essential.

Pass: choose your GPS/city station, load TLEs, find a high-elevation pass.

Record: on supported Chromium desktops use user-authorized WebUSB. Firefox/Safari or unattended recordings require the optional local receiver and its installed tools.

Decode: Robot36 WAV/CU8 candidates can be attempted locally in the common web app; Meteor LRPT currently requires optional SatDump in the native companion. Public APRS messages require a real packet decoder.

2 · Your TV tuner is not a satellite SDR

Hauppauge WinTV-soloHD 01589: receives terrestrial/cable digital TV (DVB-T/T2/C). It is not an open I/Q receiver for ISS SSTV, Meteor LRPT or raw CubeSat telemetry.

Keep it in the Live TV / DVB module. Its portable TV antenna is not a substitute for an appropriate ~137 MHz weather-satellite V-dipole/QFH. An adapter does not change tuner support.

For real Earth images: Meteor-M is a more relevant RF target than commemorative ISS SSTV. The discontinued NOAA-15/18/19 APT series must be treated as an archive, not an active station.

Sources: Hauppauge product specification · NOAA decommissioning.
